Recover a hop address

Lost the browser, or the server? Your emergency kit and your bc1p address rebuild the hop address and its spending rules. A hop address made for depositing to an exchange also needs the exchange's deposit address, which your kit lists. This page is one self-contained file. Save it and it works offline, with the server gone.

To claim, the wallet of your bc1p address spends the hidden script <D> OP_CHECKSIG, where D is the key inside that address, signing with its tweaked private key. Bitcoin Core can do this: in the spending rules above, replace D's key in pk(…) with that tweaked private key as WIF, import the result into a new blank wallet with importdescriptors, and sendall to your address. No wallet works out the tweaked key from a seed. The source code's just rescue command does, offline, and prints these commands for you. Read how it works.
